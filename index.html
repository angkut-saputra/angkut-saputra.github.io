Working Papers

Information Friction and Optimal Monetary Policy in a Non-Linear Setting

2026  |  Working Paper  |  with Sarah Jenkins

We develop a heterogeneous agent New Keynesian model with rational inattention. We demonstrate that information frictions interact with non-linearities in the Phillips curve to alter the optimal trajectory of interest rates during inflationary episodes.

[PDF] [Code]

Repeated Games with Endogenous Network Formation

2025  |  Revise & Resubmit, Journal of Economic Theory

This paper examines a setting where agents play a repeated game while simultaneously forming the network over which they interact. We characterize the set of strongly perfect equilibria and show how network density affects cooperation.

[PDF] [Appendix]

Publications

Wage Rigidity and Labor Market Dynamics

2023  |  American Economic Review, 113(4): 1021-55

An empirical analysis of downward nominal wage rigidity using administrative panel data. We provide new estimates of the friction cost and its macroeconomic implications during recessions.

About

Biography

I am an economics researcher specializing in economic theory and quantitative macroeconomics. My work primarily involves developing theoretical models to understand the implications of information frictions, and applying these models to questions in monetary policy and labor economics.

Currently, I am working on projects relating to bounded rationality in dynamic stochastic environments and endogenous network formation in strategic interactions.
